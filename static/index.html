How It Works

  1. Add sender accounts with Gmail App Passwords.
  2. Add peer accounts with just an email address (no password needed).
  3. Create templates for your warm-up emails.
  4. Create campaigns linking a sender domain, sender account, peers, and template.
  5. Activate campaigns. The scheduler queues emails during active hours.
